Hold up your phone, and Sternstunde shows the stars, the constellations, the planets, and the Milky Way in that direction – over the mountains as they really are, and without a network.

DUSK
After sunset the sky gets dark slowly. First the planets and the brightest stars appear, then more and more. Sternstunde shows the sky as your eyes see it at that moment.

CONSTELLATIONS
All 88 constellations, with their lines and names, and 5000 stars down to the faintest that the eye sees under a dark sky. Tap a star, a planet, the moon, or a constellation for its name, its brightness, its height, and its rise and set.

THE REAL HORIZON
Most sky apps use a flat horizon at 0°. In the mountains, a star rises much later. Sternstunde knows the terrain around you, so its times of rise and set are the ones you see.

PLANETS AND THE MOON
The planets move against the stars, week by week. The moon shows its phase. "Find" searches the stars, the planets, and the constellations, and an arrow leads you to the object.

THE MILKY WAY
The Milky Way needs a dark sky: no sun, no moon, and its bright center high above the horizon. Sternstunde shows all three.

ALIGN WITH A STAR
The compass of a phone is often wrong by 10° or more. Tap one bright star or a planet, and Sternstunde puts the whole sky where it really is. It also shows how far it can trust the heading.

RED LIGHT
The red light keeps your eyes adapted to the dark.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
Sternstunde works without a network. Keep the terrain of a place offline before you go. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and the app has no server of its own. It only downloads the map and terrain tiles of the area that you look at.
