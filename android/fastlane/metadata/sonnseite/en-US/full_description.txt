Hold up your phone, and Sonnseite draws the path of the sun on the camera image. Point at a window, a balcony, or a gap between two roofs, and Sonnseite tells you when the sun will be there – today, or on any day of the year.

THE DAY
Each day, the sun follows one path across your sky. Sonnseite draws this path with the hours, so you can see where the sun is at any time of the day.

THE YEAR
In June the path is high and long. In December it is low and short. The dashed lines show the two solstices, and the path of every other day is between them.

THE FLAT
Made for the moment you visit a flat. Point the phone at the window, and Sonnseite shows when the sun is in that direction – today, and on which days of the year.

THE REAL HORIZON
Sonnseite knows the terrain around you. In a valley, the sun rises later and sets earlier, and Sonnseite shows the sunrise and the sunset that you really see.

THE NIGHT
At night, Sonnseite shows the moon with its phase and its path.

A COMPASS YOU CAN TRUST INDOORS
Steel in buildings disturbs the compass of a phone. Sonnseite knows how far it can trust the heading, and it shows that. Tap the sun, the moon, or a landmark once, and the path is in the correct place.

PRECISE WHERE IT MATTERS
The position of the sun agrees with NASA's JPL Horizons to better than 0.02°. Sonnseite uses the lens calibration of the camera, the World Magnetic Model, and a filter that combines the gyroscope, the magnetometer, and your alignments.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
Sonnseite works without a network, with an offline map of the world to pick a place. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and the app has no server of its own. It downloads the map and terrain tiles of the area that you look at, and it asks the geocoder of your phone for the name of the place.
