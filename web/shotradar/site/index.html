01From where I am

Way A · I am here, or I will be

What can I shoot from here?

Shotradar reads the real skyline of your spot and marks its peaks. It lists the subjects that rise or set behind a peak, and each subject alone over the horizon.

    Way B · I have a landmark

    What do you want in the frame?

    The moon, the Milky Way, the sun, or a bright planet. Each one follows its own path across the sky, and Shotradar calculates all of them for any day and any place.

    Pick the peak

    Tap a summit, a tower, or a church on the map. Shotradar takes the height from the terrain data, and you can add the height of a building.

    05The spot

    Where must I stand?

    Or turn the question around. For a date, Shotradar draws the line of places from which the moon is behind the summit. Where the ground has the right height, there is your spot. As the moon rises, the line turns.

    17:08 CET
    The spot: 7.35 km from the summit, at 2482 m.

    06The check

    Can you see it from there?

    A ridge in front can hide the summit. Shotradar follows the line of sight over the terrain, and it checks the light: the sun, the twilight, and the moon.

      Plan your own shot

      Made for the plan, and for the place

      Precise

      The moon, the sun, and the planets come from Astronomy Engine. The moon is near, so its direction changes with your place by up to 1°: Shotradar calculates it for each point of the line.

      The real terrain

      The heights of the terrain decide where the moon appears and whether a ridge is in the way. Shotradar uses elevation data, not a flat horizon at 0°.

      Offline

      The web app needs no installation and works offline after the first visit, so the plan is with you on the mountain.

      Free and open

      No account, no ads, and no subscription. The code is open source, and you can check each calculation.
