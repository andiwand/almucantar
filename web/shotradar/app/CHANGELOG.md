# Changelog

The changes in the PWA shotradar that a user notices. Each release has the tag
`shotradar-pwa-vX.Y.Z`. The rules are the same as in `core/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Unreleased

- The first version, a prototype of the photo planner: pick the subject (the moon, the Milky Way,
  the sun, or a planet), tap the landmark on a map of the terrain, then plan. "I stand here" finds
  the dates and times of the next 12 months when the subject is behind the landmark, with the phase
  of the moon, the light of the sky, and a check of the view over the terrain. "Where must I
  stand?" draws the line of places for a time, and the spots where the ground puts the landmark in
  front of the subject; a full dot has a clear view.
