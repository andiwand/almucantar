# Changelog

The changes in the PWA of starradar that a user notices. Each release has the tag
`starradar-pwa-vX.Y.Z`. The rules are the same as in `core/CHANGELOG.md`.

## Unreleased

- The first version: the stars to magnitude 6 (size from the brightness, color from the
  temperature), the 88 constellations with their lines and names, the planets, the moon with its
  phase, and the Milky Way, in the direction of the phone or in a sky view that you drag. The sky
  gets dark with the twilight, so the stars come out in the order that the eye sees them.
- The real horizon: the terrain of the place hides the stars behind the mountains, and a tapped
  object shows its rise and set over the terrain.
- Tap a star, a planet, the moon, or a constellation for its name, brightness, height, and rise
  and set. "Find" searches the stars, the planets, and the constellations, and an arrow at the
  border of the screen leads to the object.
- "Align": tap a bright star, a planet, or the moon to correct the heading of the compass.
- The time panel: another date and time, the constellation lines, the terrain, and a red light
  that keeps the eyes adapted to the dark.
- The place panel of sunradar: search a place or tap it on the offline map, and keep the terrain
  of a place offline. The app works offline after the first visit.
- A tapped star without a proper name shows its number in the Bright Star Catalogue (HR). Before,
  it showed its place in the list of the app as an HR number, which was wrong.
