Privacy

This policy applies to the apps Sonnseite, Sternstunde, and Alidade on Android, iOS, and the web. In short: the apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server of their own. They collect no personal data.

Who is responsible

Andreas Stefl, Austria, develops the apps. For questions about this policy, open an issue on GitHub.

Your location

The apps calculate the sky for a place. With your permission, the app reads the approximate location of your device. You can also pick a place on the map, and then the app does not need the permission. The app stores the last place on your device, so it can start with it. The app does not send your location to us, because there is no server of the apps.

Your camera

Sonnseite and Sternstunde can show the sky on top of the camera image. Alidade does not use the camera. The app uses the image only on your device, to show it and, in Sonnseite, to find the sun in it. The app does not record or store the image, and it does not send the image anywhere.

The services that the apps use

To show the map and the real horizon, the app downloads tiles from these services. A tile covers an area, so the service can see which area you look at, and it sees the IP address of your device, as every server on the internet does. The app sends no other data to them.

  • The map tiles of OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), under the privacy policy of the OpenStreetMap Foundation.
  • The terrain of Copernicus DEM GLO-90, from the public data of Amazon Web Services (copernicus-dem-90m.s3.amazonaws.com), under the privacy notice of AWS.

On Android, the app asks the geocoder of your device for the name of the town at your place. The geocoder belongs to the operating system (on most phones, Google), and it can send the coordinates to its provider.

The web apps are on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the IP address of your device when your browser loads a page, under the privacy statement of GitHub.

The data on your device

The app stores its settings, the last place, the places that you keep offline, and a cache of the terrain tiles on your device. Remove the app, or clear its data, to delete all of it.

Children

The apps are for a general audience. They collect no data of anyone, also not of children.
