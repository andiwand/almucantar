almucantar

Free, open-source apps for the sky. One core calculates the sun, the moon, the stars, the planets, and the real horizon of the terrain; each app asks it one question.

Sonnseite

When is the sun at this place in the sky? The path of the sun on your camera image.

Sternstunde

Point your phone at the night sky: the stars, the constellations, the planets, and the Milky Way, over the real horizon.

Alidade

Plan the shot: from where and when is the moon, the Milky Way, or the sun behind this peak?

An almucantar is a circle of equal altitude in the sky. The source is on GitHub (MPL-2.0). Privacy
